# Dashboard surface brief

Scope: authenticated FinTrack application shell and dashboard, with the visual system extending to transaction history, fixed expenses, goals, categories and settings. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: individual users checking their financial position, recording activity and anticipating monthly risks. The task is to understand balance, movement, goals and upcoming obligations within seconds, then act without losing context.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/dashboard-margem-a.png` (A — Painel contínuo).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet financial field lets state emerge only where attention is required; it refuses the category-default grid of floating metric cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cloud-white continuous surfaces, slate typography, hairline rules and a narrow mint–rose–violet spectral edge. Regions join into one canvas; controls are compact, exact and lightly outlined. Color is evidence, never decoration.

STORY: The user first reads the current balance and month trajectory, then connects categories to goals and upcoming bills, and finally acts through a transaction or a deeper view. Every summary remains traceable to underlying entries.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim navigation rail anchors the left. The upper central field carries the greeting, month control and three aligned totals. A wide cash-flow trace dominates the middle. Distribution and a contextual observation sit below, while a right observation rail holds risk, goals and upcoming fixed expenses. The primary action remains visible in the navigation.

FORM: Margem espectral, approved challenger from seed `72f86bc0`; composition A, Panel contínuo. Signature interaction: the spectral edge and trace respond together to the selected period, with the active state traveling through summary, graph and relevant observation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: Brazilian Portuguese UI, synthetic demonstration data only where needed, WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, desktop priority with functional mobile adaptation, no status conveyed by color alone.
